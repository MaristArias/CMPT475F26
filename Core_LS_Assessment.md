# Core/LS Assessment

## Goals

The goals of the Core/Liberal Studies Program are to develop values awareness, critical thinking, integrative learning, and writing proficiency. The goal of this essay is for you to demonstrate all four competencies by reflecting on your education in Computer Science.

## Assignment

Write a reflective essay of **3–5 pages** describing your journey into Computer Science and examining how studying the discipline has influenced the way you understand technology, responsibility, and the wider world.

Your essay should address both of the following questions as parts of a unified reflection, not as a checklist of separate answers:

1. **Has learning to think from a Computer Science perspective changed your personal values, ethical perspectives, or beliefs? Why or why not?**

   You might consider how your education has shaped your thinking about privacy, security, artificial intelligence, automation, accessibility, fairness, intellectual property, environmental impact, or the responsibilities of those who create software.

2. **What other fields of study have exposed you to perspectives that differ from those commonly encountered in Computer Science? Which perspectives have held greater influence for you, and why?**

   Relevant fields might include philosophy, psychology, sociology, business, economics, political science, communication, mathematics, the natural sciences, literature, or the arts. Consider what happens when technical goals—such as efficiency, accuracy, scalability, or innovation—come into tension with human, social, or ethical concerns.

## Developing Your Reflection

A strong essay will connect your ideas to specific experiences. These might include:

- A course, reading, discussion, or assignment
- A software project or technical design decision
- An internship, job, research experience, or team collaboration
- An encounter with an ethical or social consequence of technology
- A failure, disagreement, or change in your thinking
- Your Capping Project and the people affected by it

For example, you might reflect on a project in which the technically easiest solution collected more user data than was necessary. How did you decide what to do? Did coursework in ethics, law, psychology, business, or another discipline change how you understood the problem? What does that experience suggest about your responsibilities as a computing professional?

## Expectations

Your essay should:

- Present a clear and thoughtful central reflection
- Use specific experiences as evidence
- Connect Computer Science with at least one other field of study
- Examine tensions or competing perspectives rather than offering simple answers
- Demonstrate awareness of the social and ethical consequences of computing
- Be organized, carefully written, and revised
- Conclude by considering how these reflections may influence your future professional decisions

The purpose of this assignment is not to determine whether Computer Science has led you to the “correct” beliefs. It is to demonstrate that you can critically examine your own development, integrate different ways of thinking, and articulate the values that will guide your work as a computing professional.
